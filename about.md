## Bio

I am the Authorities & Entity Management Librarian at Stanford University Libraries. I work within the Metadata Services department with colleagues that create, maintain, and clean library resource metadata across our collections and branch libraries. I primarily work with linked open data ecosystems like Blue Core, Sinopia, Share-VDE, and Wikidata. Our library runs the FOLIO library system, and works with a variety of tools for MARC and BIBFRAME data creation and maintenance. I serve on several local, national, and international committees to advance linked data standards, specifications, and best practices. This work helps advance Stanford's linked data and open metadata initiatives. In my free time, I love to bake, read, visit family, and spend time outdoors.
